The topic sentence

✗The Bone Sparrow is about a boy who lives in a detention camp.
✓Fraillon presents the camp as a place where a child has no power over his own life.

The subject of the weak one is the book. The subject of the strong one is an idea, and the verb — presents, suggests, reveals, exposes — does analytical work. Never is about.

Two paragraphs

TFraillon presents the camp as a place where a child has no power over his own life.
EWhen Beaver catches Subhi at the fence, Subhi's throat goes "as dry as the dirt" before Beaver has even touched him.
EThe fear arrives in his body first, which shows the reader that Beaver's power does not need words or violence to work.
LBy putting the fear in Subhi's body, Fraillon shows that power in the camp decides even what a child can say.

Chapter 5

TFraillon also presents imagination as the one thing the camp cannot take.
EThe novel opens at night, when the dirt outside turns into "a beautiful ocean", "as red as the sun and as deep as the sky".
EThe camp's own dirt becomes something beautiful, which lets the reader see the camp the way Subhi sees it.
LBy starting the novel inside Subhi's imagination, Fraillon shows the reader the person before the prisoner.
