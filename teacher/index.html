Print and hand out

  • A3 writing booklet
    One sheet per student, double-sided, folded once. Three cycles into a sentence, each on a different thread, plus the wall and the language glossary.
    Goes with the analysis workshop
  • A3 writing booklet · support
    Same sheets. Plain front page with the three examples only. Each cycle's draft is a coloured sentence frame with the fixed parts printed.
    Goes with the analysis workshop
  • Rotation stations
    Nine A3 station posters — three quotes, three ideas, three effects — plus a map sheet per student for drawing the links.
    Goes with the three ways into a sentence
  • TEEL sheet · imagination
    What an essay is, the paragraph in bars, a worked paragraph to colour, a frame for friendship on the back.
    Goes with TEEL · imagination and friendship
  • TEEL sheet · power
    The same sheet for kinds of power. A worked paragraph on the Jackets to colour, a frame for another kind on the back.
    Goes with TEEL · kinds of power
  • TEEL sheet · purpose
    One purpose as the prompt. A worked paragraph on learning their names to colour, a frame for another way on the back.
    Goes with TEEL · how Fraillon does it
  • Planning three paragraphs
    Six prompts, three a side. For each one, what the three paragraphs could be about and one piece of evidence each.
    Goes with TEEL · imagination and friendship
  • Essay sheet
    Intro and conclusion with gaps, paragraph A printed to copy, frames for B and C.
    Goes with the letters and the ladder
  • Essay sheet · EAL
    Word list first, shorter sentences, one sentence per line in the frames.
    Goes with the letters and the ladder
  • Essay sheet · low literacy
    Bigger print, paragraph B only, a theme and a quote to pick from on the page.
    Goes with the letters and the ladder
  • Essay sheet · extension
    TEEAL. Paragraph A gives only the T and L, the evidence sentences are theirs. B and C from the letters alone.
    Goes with the letters and the ladder
