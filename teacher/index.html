Assessment · two options, one to choose

  • Option A · a folio of paragraphs Task sheet, folio cover sheet and the five-band rubric. Three paragraphs across the sessions; the student nominates the one that is marked.
  • Idea posters Six A3 landscape sheets — imprisonment, freedom, imagination, storytelling, loneliness, friendship and family — an idea across the top and the rest open for the quotes. Collated and scanned, they become the booklet for the sitting.
  • Quote hunt One per student. Their chapter or two, and every quote they found: the words, the chapter, which poster it went on, what it shows.
  • Folio task 1 The prompt, paragraph 1 written out to colour, an introduction with the first idea in, then their own paragraphs and the self assessment.
  • Folio task 2 The second sitting. Same shape, same self assessment, a new prompt.
  • Folio task 3 The last sitting. Same shape, and the self assessment they have used twice already.
  • Option B · one text response Task sheet and the same rubric. A preparation period on the planning sheet, then one period: intro, one or two paragraphs, conclusion.
