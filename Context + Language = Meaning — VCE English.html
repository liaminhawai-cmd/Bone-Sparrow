Rear Window

Feature film, 1954 · dir. Alfred Hitchcock

Confined to a wheelchair with a broken leg, photographer L.B. Jefferies passes the time watching his neighbours through his apartment window — and becomes convinced he has witnessed a murder. A study in voyeurism, complicity, and the ethics of watching.

The Memory Police

Novel, 1994 · Yoko Ogawa (trans. Stephen Snyder, 2019)

On a mysterious island, objects gradually vanish — and with them, the islanders’ memories. A secret police force hunts those who remember what has been erased. A fable about state control, memory, erasure, and what it means to hold on to the past.
